type a request. the lab hides it among decoy requests, pads every request to a fixed size class, gives them random arrival times, and commits to the decoys with a hash. then you look at the batch the way a provider would.

this is the v0.1 client logic, running in your browser. nothing you type leaves this page: no request reaches any model, network or server.

    LIMITS OF v0.1

    • local only. there are no miners, gateways or contracts yet, so nothing is paid and nothing is sent.
    • the decoy generator uses fixed templates. a provider with a trained detector could spot many of them; making decoys match real conversation patterns is open work.
    • the audit here only checks hashes. the planned audit also checks that each decoy reached a gateway.
    • timing is simulated. a real client must also hide when you type and send.
